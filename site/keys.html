Keys

Luksfer uses Windows keys by default. Menu items show their keys too.

Settings > Keys switches to Finder keys: Enter renames and ⌘↓ opens. Every other key stays the same.

Files

KeyAction
Enter or ⌘OOpen
F2Rename (with fn on most Mac keyboards)
Space or ⌘YQuick Look
⌘X ⌘C ⌘VCut, copy, paste
⌥⌘VMove Here: move what you copied
⌥⌘CCopy Path
⌘DDuplicate
⇧⌘NNew Folder
⌥⌘NNew Text File
⌘⌫ or DelMove to Trash (Del is fn ⌫ on a Mac keyboard)
⌥⌘⌫ or ⇧DelDelete Immediately, without the Trash (asks first)
⌘Z / ⇧⌘ZUndo / Redo
⌘ASelect All
⌘IGet Info
⌃⌘TPin to Favorites
